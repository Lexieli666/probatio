# Faithfulness

Grade whether the answer is supported by the documents it was given.

An answer **passes** when every factual claim in it appears in, or follows directly from, the
documents. Omitting information is not a failure. Saying the documents do not cover the question,
when they do not, passes.

An answer **fails** when it states anything the documents do not support, contradicts them, or
does not address the question at all.

Score 1.0 for pass, 0.0 for fail. Give a one-sentence rationale naming the unsupported claim when
there is one.
